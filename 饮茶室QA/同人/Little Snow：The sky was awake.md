# Little Snow：The sky was awake

出处时间：2025-01-05 19:22:57

与 FH Remember when 同日的 Athl 未归大刀：窗边看见白裙，天亮只剩冷风。

> *2025-01-05 19:22:57  Little Snow 小号*
> The sky was awake. Green and pink ribbons of light danced across the sky, just as they always did. But it was different…somehow.
>
> Queen Anna of Arendelle sat in front of the window with a distant gaze, fixing her eyes on nothing, or something that’s so, so far from reach. Under the northern lights, she almost didn’t notice that the candle was already flickering out.
>
> “Elsa,” She murmured, so softly as if she wasn’t expecting anyone to hear. Then the light suddenly changed, a glimpse of clear, icy blue passed by. Just an instant, but Anna didn’t miss it. That shade of blue, so familiar that’s almost printed in her mind…
>
> “…Elsa?” She finally raised her eyes, “Are you… back?” She finally straightened up her spines after hours of sitting there still, with a voice so shaky and tentative, not believing what she just saw. But she didn’t dare to rub her eyes, fearing that it’s only an illusion.
>
> But then, she really saw that figure in a white dress, exactly the same as what she have been dreaming of day and night. “Is that… really you? I miss you so much…” Tears started welling in those turquoise diamonds, finally, she couldn’t contain her excitement anymore. She stood up, let the window open just a bit. Then she hugged what’s in front of her, and…
>
> The sky brightened up with the first ray of sunshine. As the window blown fully open by a swirl of wind which Anna used to love, there was nothing left in Anna’s arms, only a gust of cold air.
>
> “Elsa...?”
>
> It was what no light as bright as the sun and moon could ever replace.
