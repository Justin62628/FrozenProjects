# FH：Our story began

出处时间：2020-05-02 02:38:01

用故事口吻复述 F1 开场误伤，不是官设新剧情。

> *2020-05-02 02:38:01  FrozenHeart*
> Our story began with the royal sisters of Arendelle, Elsa and Anna.
> It was a beautiful night, the Aurora Borealis
> danced in the sky.
> The younger Princess, Anna, couldn't sleep. So she woke her sister, asking her to play using her magic.
> Anna pulled Elsa down to the great hall, where Elsa turned the place into a winter wonderland.
> The sound of their laughter filled the hall as they enjoyed Elsa's amazing gift-- the power to create ice and snow.
> The fun did not last long, however. Caught up in the exhilaration of their games, Elsa lost her footing on ice and accidentally sent a bolt of magic at Anna, and hit her directly on the head, knocking her unconcious.
> Realizing what she had done, Elsa cried out for her parents for help.
> Seeing that Anna's body have turned ice-cold, the king and queen immediately left to seek the trolls for help.
> The elder troll, Grand Pabbie agreed to provide aid when they arrived at the Valley of living rocks. He removed all magic, even memories of magic from Anna in order to heal her, resulting in Anna having no idea of Elsa's powers.
> Fearing she may hurt her sister again, Elsa ceased all contact with Anna, and locked herself away in her room. The King ordered the castle to close off until his daughter may have full control over her strange powers, he also lectured Elsa to wear gloves to supress her gift.
