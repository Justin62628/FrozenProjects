# FH：You have no right to claim Elsa

出处时间：2020-05-19 22:16:29

2020-05-19 原文。后来多次重发（2020-08 / 2020-09 / 2024-08），回指本页。

> *2020-05-19 22:16:29  FrozenHeart*
> *↪ 2042064846：明天请大家都不要在网上说艾莎是自己的了，我怀里的艾莎要是看到了我又得安慰她几天*
> @熊猫菌  You have no right to claim Elsa's yours, nor do any Frozen fans in the world. Elsa is a spirit of nature, the bridge between the magic of nature and humans. She is, in the Frozen world, of the same importance of Jesus in Christianity. We may only look up upon her, marveling at the powers she holds and the complexity of her character. Her name is not to be sullied by such blasphemous use. Even in the fictional worlds, it is my belief that noone, human or otherwise, may be worthy enough to ask for her hand, exept for Anna, perhaps. As none but her sister understand the pain and internal struggle she underwent. Yet no evidence may show that Anna have any sign of being romantically attracted by a female. Furthermore, Elsa as the fifth spirit, holds great responsibility on her shoulders, yet no other existence as I know of may be able to share this burden with her, since she is the one and only who understand both human and magic so well. So, the next time you daydream about Elsa sitting on your laps, consider first，whether you are worthy.
