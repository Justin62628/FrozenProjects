# FH：第五灵与Noaidi中介

出处时间：2020-04-04 18:29:32

主张：用萨米 Noaidi（萨满）当透镜：第五灵是人与灵的中介，用牺牲/仪式重建两边平衡，不是「没事的吉祥物」。提出者 FrozenHeart。群：❄️冰学奇谈会议室。日常真空见 [[饮茶室QA/冰学讨论/第五灵日常职责真空]]。

> *2020-04-04 18:29:32  FrozenHeart*
Noaidis, Sámi shaman, were traditionally the healers and protectors. Noaidis are said to have the role of mediator between humans and the spirits. To undertake this mediation, the noaidi communicated with the spirit world, asking what sacrifice needed to be made by a person so that they might return to good health, be successful in their hunt for food, and even for good weather. Sacrifices were designed by the noaidi to reestablish the balance between the mortal and immortal worlds.

Noaidi 还处理需要智慧的事：治病、护孩、决策、保护驯鹿。史料来自法庭记录、传说、出土工具和传教士报告；被当作「巫术」处死，更像是王权消灭异见。这和「第五灵只在出事时出场、平时没有形体」的抱怨对得上：中介本来就该有可叙述的日常职分，F2 没写出来。
